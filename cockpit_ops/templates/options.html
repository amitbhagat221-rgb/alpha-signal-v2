At your capital
One calculator per index. Each trade is re-sized to as many whole lots as that index's capital covers on expiry day (entry margin + the exchange's extra 2% per sold option); the margin tiles break that peak into SPAN, exposure and the expiry-day extra for the index's latest trade. P&L scales per lot, costs included; brokerage is per order, so this slightly overstates costs at size. Live, the Tuesday 15:08 NIFTY buy-back lets one pot carry both; the paper book holds both to the Tuesday close.

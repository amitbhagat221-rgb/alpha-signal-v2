## 1 Summary and verdict

**The ingredients mostly exist; the gating idea mostly does not survive the project's own evidence.** The repo already stores an EOD option chain for NIFTY/BANKNIFTY from 2019-01-01 (1,913 chain days) and for 273 stock symbols from 2024-07-15 (554 days), an in-house Black-76 IV rollup (`fno_iv_history`), a nearest-expiry OI/PCR/max-pain rollup, a point-in-time market reading (`option_state.readings`), participant-wise OI from 2022-01-03, 2.6 M timestamped BSE filings from 2018, and a strangle engine shared by backtest and paper book (`option_book.py`). What is missing is small and mostly backfillable: a ban-list/MWPL table, lot sizes, a scheduled-event table, per-event implied-move history, a stock-aware chain reader, and the forward-only items (EOD bid/ask, Kite margins).

**Verdict on "a setup is a conjunction of gates; one failed gate zeroes it."** For short index premium this is contradicted by the project's record. Every de-risking gate tested on the proven static strangle lost money: the event veto, the VIX > 25 veto, and all six plan-0023 readings (premium ≤ realised, trend, skew, inverted term, crowded PCR, rupee/oil shock) — the SKIP policies cost −0.16 % and −0.19 % of margin per trade (t −5.9, −5.0) and the top-quintile "scary" readings earned the most (0.73 vs 0.49 % per trade) ([plan 0023:63-70](../plans/0023-dynamic-option-setups.md); [ADR 0068:24-34](../decisions/0068-no-dynamic-filters-on-option-strangle.md)). So the only gates this report keeps for the short-premium setup are **validity gates** (calendar, chain completeness, strike availability, lot/margin), not risk vetoes. For long convexity and single-stock direction the project has no evidence either way; their gates are hypotheses with placeholder thresholds and a pre-registered test each.

**What is proven, what is not.** Proven on real prices, net of modelled costs: the 0.05-delta NIFTY strangle sold two sessions before the Tuesday expiry — 378 trades 2019-02→2026-10, 24.6 %/yr of margin, Sharpe 2.88, t 7.9, worst trade −8.9 %, max drawdown −12.8 %, no losing year ([study:128-132](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)); picked from a 32-cell grid after the data was seen, so it is on a forward paper test, not in production ([ADR 0067:9](../decisions/0067-option-premium-paper-book.md)). Not proven: any defined-risk version (1 % wings failed, worst −95.5 %), any long-premium setup, any directional single-stock option setup, and any LARGE-cap directional edge at all (walk-forward OOS t 0.73 in LARGE). The only within-F&O cross-sectional factor that survives the multiple-testing haircut is `iv_skew_25d` in MID (t 4.10, BY-FDR p 0.0243).

**Setups kept: three, not four.** (A) defined-risk short premium on NIFTY, framed as a wide-wing *hypothesis* beside the proven naked strangle; (B) long convexity on NIFTY ahead of a dated catalyst (RBI, Budget, election count); (C) a 2 %-wide bull vertical on a MID-tier F&O stock, direction from the equity model plus skew. The results-season volatility setup is **folded** into B's ingredient list: its direction is unknown (the 11-of-12 "implied > realised" stock result compares a 20-day implied move with a one-day realised move on six hand-picked macro days, not results days), US evidence flips sign from +3.34 % gross to −11.8 % net, and stock-chain history is 27 months with no slippage measurement.

**External record in one line.** Winners bought convexity when it was cheap with a dated reason (Universa, Ackman); blow-ups sold an uncapped tail on margin in a compressed-vol regime (LJM, XIV, OptionSellers). SEBI: 93 % of 1.07 crore individual F&O traders lost in FY22–24, aggregate > ₹1.8 lakh crore, 92 % of losses in options. The only cost-aware Indian short-vol study (Pillai, monthly, 2015–2025) finds every variant negative net [SNIPPET-ONLY, unverified]; no Indian implied-vs-realised study for RBI/Budget/elections exists, so the eleven-episode replay in section 3b is the only Indian evidence here, and it is selection-biased.

**Corrections to the earlier report (section 7)** — the material ones: its premium figures were model values and the real chain disagrees (the model predicted losing years for exp2; there are none); margin is 11.8 % measured on Kite, not 12 % assumed, plus 2 % ELM on expiry day; a round trip on one NIFTY lot at ₹50 costs ₹54.91 after 1 Apr 2026 (₹53.28 before); STT on option sales is 0.15 %; NIFTY's lot is 65 (not 25 or 75); every NSE expiry is a Tuesday since 1 Sep 2025 and BANKNIFTY has had no weekly since 13 Nov 2024; the "10 %/yr" figure has no published Indian support; and since 3 Aug 2026 index settlement keys off a closing auction that SEBI may unwind by end-October.

**Pre-registered shortlist (section 5):** ten items, in order — expiry-session calendar; wing-credit-retained (the defined-risk test); scheduled-event table; event implied move; event realised move; ban-list/MWPL flag; stock lot size; stock option liquidity; stock CPIV spread; results implied-vs-realised. **Archive now:** EOD option chain with bid/ask/IV (`option-chain-v3`), Kite quotes and basket margins, the forward lot-size file, the ban list going forward, the NSE event calendar.

### 2.1 Setup A — Defined-risk short premium on NIFTY (wide-wing hypothesis beside the proven strangle)

**Thesis (two sentences).** The static 0.05-delta strangle sold two sessions before the Tuesday weekly expiry has earned 24.6 %/yr of margin on real prices 2019–2026 with no losing year and a worst trade of −8.9 % ([study:128-132](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)); its only unmanaged exposure is an uncapped tail. The defined-risk variant buys far wings purely as a sizing/tail device whose cost is known to be high — 1 % wings risked the whole margin on a 1.5 % move (exp1 condor worst −95.5 %, [study:33-38](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)) and the replay's 0.10-delta/1 %-wing condors hit full max loss in 4 of 10 episodes — so the open question is whether a much wider wing keeps enough of the credit to be worth its margin saving.

**What this setup is NOT.** It is not a re-proposal of the six rejected filters nor of the event/VIX vetoes. Those readings are logged for the record and never gate an entry (row A6).

### 2.4 The fourth setup (results-season volatility): folded, not built

It is folded into Setup B's ingredient list for four reasons from the notes. (1) Its direction is unknown: the replay's stock monthly straddle showed implied > realised in 11 of 12 stock-episodes, but that compares a 20–25-day implied move with a single day's realised move on six macro days (not results days), which "understates how much of its premium the event consumed" (section 3b, KQ4 note). (2) The only quantitative anchors are US and flip sign on costs: ATM earnings straddles +3.34 % gross ([Gao–Xing–Zhang](https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/anticipating-uncertainty-straddles-around-earnings-announcements/7B34877AD5E06304BA3C55FBA3219FDD)) vs −11.8 % net when bought at the ask and sold at the bid ([Govindaraj et al.](https://wallstreethorizon.com/upload/SSRN-id2501750_govindaraj.pdf)). (3) Stock chains exist for 554 days only, with physical settlement, ban-list exposure and no measured slippage. (4) The hypothesis budget is ≤ 10 per month ([ADR 0051:27](../decisions/0051-roadmap-to-90-decisions-d1-d12.md)). So shortlist item 10 measures `results_implied_vs_realised` over every F&O results print since 2024-07-15 first; its sign decides whether a long (B-on-a-stock) or a short (A-on-a-stock) results setup is pre-registered later.

### 3b Indian episode replay

Method (from the replay notes, read-only replay of `config.DB_PATH` opened `mode=ro` by `scratchpad/replay/stage1.py`, `stage2.py`, `stage3.py`; nothing written, no harvester or backtest run). "Prior evening" = the last `fno_bhav` NIFTY session before the event day. Closes from `macro_history.nifty50`; OHL from `nse_index_history('NIFTY 50')`; IV/skew/term from `fno_iv_history`; PCR from `fno_pcr_history`; positioning from `fii_dii_positioning`; VIX from `macro_history.india_vix` (percentile = share of VIX values strictly before the prior date, since 2015-06-25). **Selection bias (stated plainly).** These dates were picked *because* the index moved, or because a scheduled event was known to be binary. Ten of eleven had |move| ≥ 0.76 % and six had |move| ≥ 3.78 %, against an unconditional NIFTY daily |move| that is far smaller. None of the counts above says how often the same gate readings occur on days when nothing happens — the base-rate study (e.g. `option_state.readings` over all 1,913 chain days) that would answer it has not been run. The eleven are also not independent of the structures' design: the 0.10Δ short strikes sit roughly 2–3.5 % from spot on a 3–7-day weekly, so any pre-selected ≥ 4 % day defeats them by construction. The project's own unselected evidence — 378 ordinary entry days — points the other way for short premium (section 3c, lesson 6).

### 3c This project's own lessons

Each paragraph is one rule, with its number and the file that records it.

**Pre-register the grid and report every cell; a cell found after looking is a hypothesis for forward days only.** The pre-registered primary (exp1 strangle, 0.05/0.10 delta, 2019-02→2024-07) failed: Sharpe 0.59/0.62, t ≈ 1.4, worst −28.1 %/−39.2 % on 2020-03-11 ([study:113-121](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)). The exp2 cell that holds up "is in the pre-registered grid but was not the primary, so treat it as a post-hoc pick" ([study:126](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)); "only new days can test it" ([ADR 0067:9](../decisions/0067-option-premium-paper-book.md)). Every setup in section 2 therefore carries a test in section 5 whose thresholds are fixed before the first number.

**Cap tier: expect nothing in LARGE.** "No LARGE factor survives the multiple-testing haircut, and neither the old nor the new set shows anything over the last 25 months" ([ADR 0063:24](../decisions/0063-weights-on-corrected-evidence.md)); walk-forward out-of-sample selection t LARGE 0.73, MID 1.92, SMALL 5.13 ([factor-audit:236](../studies/factor-audit-2026-10.md)); "LARGE: hold the index" ([names-per-tier:6](../studies/names-per-tier-2026-10.md)); 21 new LARGE candidates failed (best t −1.93, [large-factors:3-5](../studies/large-factors-2026-10.md)) and the re-audit of ~100 factors found "still no new LARGE factor" ([large-reaudit:1-3](../studies/large-reaudit-2026-10-10.md)). F&O stocks are almost all LARGE/MID, so a single-stock option setup cannot borrow direction from LARGE, and an index setup is a LARGE-cap bet with no proven directional edge — which is why Setup C is MID-only and Setup B is a vol bet, not a direction bet.

**Multiple testing: haircut every t for the hypotheses already spent.** "|t| ≥ 2.5 is necessary, not sufficient. The Bonferroni bar at our M is |t| ≈ 4.2; BY-FDR passes only 8 of 269" ([ADR 0043:22-23](../decisions/0043-multiple-testing-aware-factor-significance.md)); on 2026-10-11 M = 295, Bonferroni bar |t| ≥ 3.94, six BY survivors, five of them wired (`python -m tools.multiple_testing`, read-only). Plan 0023 set its bar at t ≥ 2.4 after a Bonferroni haircut for six hypotheses ([plan 0023:42](../plans/0023-dynamic-option-setups.md)); guardrails are ≤ 10 hypotheses a month and dead ends stay dead ([ADR 0051:27](../decisions/0051-roadmap-to-90-decisions-d1-d12.md)). The dead cells for options are: exp1, daily roll, 1 %-wing condors, event veto, VIX veto, the six dynamic filters. None is re-proposed here.

**Point-in-time discipline is specific.** The label enters the session after the signal — entering at the eval close "credited factors with a move nobody could trade" ([pit.py:377-378](../../pit.py)); a past date carries the tier of that date ("of the true top 100 by market cap in June 2021, 39 are not LARGE today", [pit.py:1466-1468](../../pit.py)); an option reading older than 7 days is not today's — "SAIL ranked on 10-03 with a skew from 09-11 on an expired contract" ([factor-audit:129](../studies/factor-audit-2026-10.md)) → `DERIVATIVE_MAX_AGE_DAYS = 7` ([pit.py:44](../../pit.py)); the worst look-ahead was a future value under an honest date (`pt_upside` t 7–9 from "the stock's REALIZED CLOSE roughly a year after the stamped date", [ADR 0045:8-13](../decisions/0045-pull-pt-upside-lookahead.md)); lag is declared, not inferred ([ADR 0053](../decisions/0053-declared-lag-not-cadence-lag.md)); the option reading already lags US-hours data by a day and uses expanding percentiles ([plan 0023:10-11](../plans/0023-dynamic-option-setups.md)); consensus estimates stay `pit_unverified` ([ADR 0071:25](../decisions/0071-consensus-beat-rate-wired-mid.md)). A setup reading at the entry close may use only what was printed by that close, with an explicit max age per derivative input (section 2, gates A5, C5).

**Hostile data: a column is named for what it holds and checked against a second source.** Tickertape's "interest" was operating expenses for months ([CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)); 44 copied trading days were deleted ([ADR 0062:16](../decisions/0062-factor-audit-fixes-one-model-change.md)); `forecast_history.price` was the realised year-ahead close (memory, ADR 0045). Option-specific: the IV rollup silently lost 495 NIFTY days to a thin-weekly fallback until 2026-10-10 ([plan 0023:59](../plans/0023-dynamic-option-setups.md)); a missing BSE day is HTTP 200 + HTML ([sources/bse_fo.py:14-15](../../sources/bse_fo.py)); on expiry day `settle` holds the index settlement ([tools/option_premium_backtest.py:17-18](../../tools/option_premium_backtest.py)); far-OTM settles are stale; `fno_bhav`'s write contract (`max_null settle 0.05`) must never be loosened to pass a harvest ([CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)). `atm_iv` was validated against India VIX to 0.1–2 vol points ([ADR 0035:9](../decisions/0035-fno-iv-derived-from-bhav.md)); every new option series in section 5 gets the same kind of second-source check.

**Index premium already prices visible risk: vetoes and dynamic filters lost money.** Event veto: skipped event trades did better than kept ones (0.26 % vs 0.15 % per trade) and the worst trade was unchanged; VIX > 25 veto helps 2019–24 (worst −28 % → −17 %) but fails the same-direction check on 2024-11→2026-10 and in the 2015–2019 model ([study:123-125](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)). Plan 0023: H1 −0.16 %/trade (t −5.9), H2 −0.02 % (−0.8), H3 +0.00 % (+0.7), H4 −0.19 % (−5.0), H5 −0.04 % (−4.4), H6 −0.07 % (−4.0), SENSEX same sign ([plan 0023:63-70](../plans/0023-dynamic-option-setups.md)). The reason: the 0.05-delta strike moves out when IV rises, and the top-quintile "scary" states earned 0.73/0.73/0.70/0.89 % vs 0.49/0.44/0.44/0.37 % per trade in the bottom quintile; "the tail did not cluster in the flagged states" ([ADR 0068:24-34](../decisions/0068-no-dynamic-filters-on-option-strangle.md)). The only sanctioned open directions are sizing *up* when premium is rich and intraday management from forward Kite minute data ([ADR 0068:18-20](../decisions/0068-no-dynamic-filters-on-option-strangle.md)).

**Trust the real chain over a VIX·√T model, and size for the surprise day.** "The VIX model was wrong about exp2. It predicted losing years; real prices show none" ([study:138](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)); the March 2020 near miss had the short put 9.4 % out of the money because VIX was 30.8 — "one more percent down would have cost a few percent of margin, not a third of it" ([study:137](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)); "sizing comes before everything else" ([study:67](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)). Every test in section 5 runs on stored real prices and reports worst trade and max drawdown as gates.

**Costs and margin are assumptions until measured.** Slippage max(0.05, 1 % of price) per leg per side and margin 12 % of forward were assumptions ([plan 0022:18](../plans/0022-option-premium-paper-book.md); [study:73-76](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)); Kite then measured 11.8 %/10.7 % ([study:155-160](../studies/option-premium-feasibility-2026-10.md)); the live-quote shadow (`option_live_quotes`, settle − bid) is the slippage measurement and has 0 rows as of 2026-10-11. The equity parallel: SMALL's edge hinges on a 150 bps cost placeholder (break-even ~95–150, [names-per-tier:26](../studies/names-per-tier-2026-10.md)). Single-stock options are thinner than NIFTY; Setup C's pass bar cannot be read until its slippage is measured.

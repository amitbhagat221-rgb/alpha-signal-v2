## Watch out
- **v3 read switch: the gate compares REAL columns at 10 significant digits** (`json_object` keeps 15; every float in `attrs` differs bit-wise from the legacy column). A reader that compares a score to a stored float bit for bit will see a difference after the flip; nothing shown carries more than 6 digits. And the mirror is only as current as the last `datamodel_sync` (end of the morning run): a producer that writes after it shows as drift until the next sync.
- **Everything in the tree was committed and pushed on 2026-10-31 on Amit's "push all changes from all sessions"** (1925335), including the options session's plan 0029 controls (its ADR follows from that session) and the 2026-10-18 Ask docs. Only runtime files under `output/`, `.archify/` and a stray `anc.js` stay untracked. The pre-push suite is the gate: 1,124 pass; it failed once on the INSERT OR REPLACE ratchet (fixed in 3566999).
- **`DATA_FAULT_IN_PICK_INPUTS` is WARN by design and will stand** until the data-engineer fixes the sources (159 stale annuals is a Tickertape harvest gap, 34 share counts are vendor errors): judge it on Sunday's memo, don't mute it.

Fixed; a `missed` list or an exception there is a finding, not noise.
- **Options: Kite's user-margins API times out at night;** basket margins still answer, so a read-only plan check at night stubs `available` / `peak_available` / `india_vix`.
- **Options: `option_book.wing_view` prices wings at the fno_bhav settle,** which can be stale on far strikes; it is a view, not what the bot pays (the bot quotes Kite at the ask).
- **Targets: 272 of 941 targets (259 SMALL) are "thin"** (< 3 analysts): the target shows with the reason, no upside, and they are out of the book's expected return. Expected, not a data loss.
